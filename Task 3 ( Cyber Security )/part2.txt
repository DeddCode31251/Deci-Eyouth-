1)
Normal:
Hello Username,

We noticed a new sign-in to your account from a Windows device in Alexandria, Egypt. If this was you, no action is required. If you don't recognize this sign-in, please open our official app or visit our official website to secure your account.

Phishing:
 URGENT SECURITY ALERT 

Your account has been HACKED! We have temporarily suspended your account. Verify your identity within the next 10 minutes or your account will be permanently deleted.

Click here immediately:
https://secure-account-verify.example

Red Flags:
- Creates panic and urgency.
- Threatens account deletion.
- Uses an unfamiliar link.
- Doesn't tell you to use the official website or app.
- May ask for your password or verification code.

2)
Normal:
Your package is out for delivery today. You can track your shipment by opening the official courier app or visiting the retailer's website where you placed the order.

Phishing:
 DELIVERY FAILED!

Your package could not be delivered due to an invalid address. Pay a $1.99 redelivery fee immediately or your package will be returned today.

Pay Now:
https://delivery-fast.example

Red Flags:
- Unexpected payment request.
- Artificial urgency ("today").
- Suspicious website.
- Uses fear to pressure you into acting quickly.
- Small payment requests are commonly used to steal credit card details.
